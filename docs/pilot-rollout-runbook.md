## Operating cadence

1. Confirm consent, roster readiness, device/browser support, and facilitator access before launch.
2. Complete the baseline during the documented baseline window.
3. Release one approved weekly module at the planned cadence.
4. Review only aggregate completion blockers and privacy-safe feedback each week.
5. Run the post-assessment during its assigned window.
6. Close the pilot only after support issues, data quality, and completion status are reviewed.

## Completion definition

A pilot is complete when the planned cohort has finished the agreed learning window, baseline and post-assessment windows have closed, open severity-one issues are resolved, and the owner has recorded completion blockers and exceptions.

## Report-ready definition

A pilot is report-ready when consent boundaries are confirmed, participant data is internally consistent, missing answers remain distinguishable from zero, duplicate submissions are resolved, feedback is de-identified, and the approved reporting owner has signed off. Pilot Insights dashboards and branded PDFs are outside Pilot Readiness v1.0.

## Support escalation

- Severity 1 — access, data isolation, security, or cohort-wide outage: stop affected activity and notify the release owner immediately.
- Severity 2 — repeated workflow failure with a safe workaround: record steps, browser/device, and time; respond the same business day.
- Severity 3 — isolated usability or content question: provide the approved workaround and include it in the weekly review.

Never request passwords, access codes, student PINs, or secret keys in support messages.
